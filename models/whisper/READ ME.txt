Speech models (Whisper) go here, one folder per model.

The easiest way is the Models page of the Subtle window (Download button).
To add one yourself, download a Whisper model from huggingface.co in the Transformers
format and put its whole folder here, e.g.
    models\whisper\whisper-large-v3\config.json, model.safetensors, tokenizer.json, ...
Then press Rescan on the Models page.
